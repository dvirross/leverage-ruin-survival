# Analysis summary: leverage, ruin and survival

Generated by `scripts/run_analysis.py` (seed 20261110). Every number below is computed from the CSV tables in this folder; full precision is in the CSVs.

## Data and conventions (read first)

- US: Kenneth R. French Data Library, daily `F-F_Research_Data_Factors_daily`: 1926-07-01 to 2026-09-30, 26,338 trading days. `MKT` = Mkt-RF + RF (value-weighted CRSP market, total return including dividends), `RF` = daily risk-free (one-month T-bill) return. Both simple daily returns, decimals.
- Japan: French `Japan_3_Factors_Daily`: 1990-07-02 to 2026-09-30, 9,458 days (the file starts on 1990-07-02, not January 1990); returns are in **US dollars** (include yen moves).
- 252 trading days per year for annualisation and financing. Simple returns; no taxes or trading costs; only daily closes are observed, so a wipe-out or a margin call is detected at a close and **gap risk inside a day is not modelled** (real losses and forced closures could be larger/earlier).
- Financing: borrowed money costs RF + spread (spread 0 or 1.5% a year, charged only on borrowing, i.e. L > 1, or on the loan of the margin kind); cash and short proceeds earn RF; no stock-borrow fee for shorts.
- Window analyses: start dates are every 21st trading day from the first day of the sample; a window of h years is the next 252*h daily returns. **Windows overlap heavily**, so the numbers are counts/shares over start dates, not independent observations; no confidence intervals are given. The number of windows is in the CSV (`n_windows`).
- 'wipe-out' = equity <= 0 at some close in the window (equity is absorbed at 0 for daily rebalancing). 'fall 50%' = equity at some close <= 50% of the window's starting equity. 'below start' = final equity < 1.
- Median/10th percentile of growth are over windows of the annualised geometric growth rate (final^(1/years) - 1; -100% if wiped out). 'Mean arith. return' is the mean over windows of (final equity - 1)/years (the arithmetic, 'it works on average' metric, per year).
- Kinds of leverage: **daily** = position reset to leverage L every close (leveraged-ETF style; `daily_rebalanced`); **loan close** = fixed loan of (L-1) per unit equity, never rebalanced, maintenance margin 25% (equity/position value < 25% at a close triggers a call), the position is closed at that close and the investor keeps the equity (which then earns RF); **loan restore** = at a call the position is cut back to the original leverage L (losses locked in) and continues. **Caveat on the maintenance rule**: a fixed loan at leverage L starts with equity/position = 1/L, so with maintenance 25% every L >= 4 is at or below the maintenance level on day 0: L = 4 is called by any down day and L = 5, 6 are called on the first close (loan close: closed after one day; loan restore: cut back to L every day, i.e. behaves like daily rebalancing). A second maintenance level of 10% (valid for every L <= 6) is therefore also reported (column `maintenance` in the CSVs).
